The people doing the work — embodied AI, assistive locomotion, and spatial perception.

01How I advise

Research is inherently a collaborative endeavor, and I have the distinct privilege of working alongside a dedicated group of emerging scholars. My primary responsibility as an advisor is to provide the necessary framework and resources to help them transition into independent problem-solvers and capable colleagues. I deeply value their diligence, technical contributions, and the steady focus they bring to our ongoing work in AI and physical systems.
